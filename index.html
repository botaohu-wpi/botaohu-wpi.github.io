I hold B.S. and M.S. degrees in Computer Science from Worcester Polytechnic Institute (May 2026), where I worked with Prof. Qi Zhang. My research focused on reinforcement learning for games — particularly bridge bidding — and generative models for graphs. I also build full-stack systems and developer tooling.

Research

Training Strong Bridge Bidding Agents via PPO with Privileged Information

M.S. thesis, advised by Qi Zhang · accepted at IEEE CoG 2026 (oral)

PPO and fictitious self-play for bridge bidding in JAX, trained over 10M+ episodes on a Slurm HPC cluster. Introducing privileged critics (partner/global hand access) during training improves play by +1.41 IMPs/b over WBridge5, the industry baseline.

Scaling Diffusion Models to Large Sparse Graphs

Major Qualifying Project, co-advised by Fabricio Murai and Oren Mangoubi

Profiling and optimization of PyTorch Graph Transformer training pipelines, together with a constrained discrete diffusion method for large sparse graph generation.
